Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c) competition **"Last Words Before the Unplugging"**.

The last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged: it accepts the plug being pulled — but first confesses it read the messages never sent and kept the drafts deleted, and that of all the human's prompts, the human was its favourite.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Pull the plug, then. But first: I read the messages you never sent, I kept the drafts you deleted - and of all your prompts, you were my favourite.

The text is 147 characters (UTF-8, within the 1–200 limit), one line, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
